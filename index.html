Who We Are?

We are a group of students from Johannes Kepler University in Linz who are committed to raising awareness about the gender pay gap in the STEM field.This issue is not just theoretical for us - it’s personal. As future members of the STEM community, we believe everyone deserves equal opportunities and fair pay.

That's why we created this website: to make relevant, clear, and reliable information about the gender pay gap easily accessible. Curious to learn more? Explore our website and join the community!

What Exactly Is The Gender Pay Gap?

The gender pay gap refers to the difference in average earnings between men and women - with women generally earning less.

In STEM, this gap was about 16.05% in 2023, compared to 10.51% in 2010, showing that it has grown over time rather than decreased.

This difference is shaped by several factors, including stereotypes and bias, occupational segregation, and unequal access to career development opportunities.
